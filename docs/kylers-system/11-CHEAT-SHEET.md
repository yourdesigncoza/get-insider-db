# Kyler's Deep Value Turnaround System -- Quick Reference

---

## The Philosophy

Buy quality businesses with temporary problems priced as permanent damage. In Kyler's words: "buy a dollar for loose change." Target 30% annual returns, hold 12 positions max, hold 1-4 years.

---

## Step 1: Find Ideas ([01-FINDING-IDEAS](01-FINDING-IDEAS.md))

- **Top-down first:** Look for underperforming sectors/industries with clear catalysts
- **Screens:** Broken charts (60-80%+ from highs), low P/FCF, low P/S
- **Sources:** Substack, YouTube, podcasts, Stock Unlock, Finviz
- **Two conditions:** Must be understandable. Must not be in secular decline.
- **Avoid:** AI, Airlines, Banks, Biotech, Car Manufacturers, Insurance, Marine Freight, Precious Metal Miners, Restaurants, Tobacco, Textiles, Trading Firms, Most Software, Video Games

---

## Step 2: First Filter ([02-FIRST-FILTER](02-FIRST-FILTER.md))

Five checks, in order:

| Check | What to Ask |
|---|---|
| **Solvency** | Any major debt issues? Can they survive long enough for the turnaround? |
| **Dilution** | Rising share count to line management's pockets? Check per-share growth. |
| **Revenue Growth** | Will revenues grow based on history + known catalysts? |
| **ROCE/ROIC** | History of efficient capital use? Red flag if chronically below ~6%. |
| **Valuation** | Cheap on P/S, P/FCF, EV/FCF, EV/EBITDA vs. own history? |

**Must show 30%+ CAGR potential to proceed.**

---

## Step 3: Competitor Comparison ([03-DEEP-ANALYSIS](03-DEEP-ANALYSIS.md))

- Compare: leverage, ROIC, margins, niches, growth, dilution, potential returns
- Goal: identify the clearly highest-quality name in the group
- **Quality priority order:** (1) Balance sheet strength, (2) Superior niche/margins, (3) Catalyst exposure
- Weaker companies stay on the list only if they offer materially higher returns

---

## Step 4: Qualitative Deep Dive ([04-QUALITATIVE-DEEP-DIVE](04-QUALITATIVE-DEEP-DIVE.md))

Five questions:

1. **Moat?** Low-cost production, switching costs, regulatory barriers, scale, brands?
2. **Management?** Positive track record? Right skills for this situation?
3. **Issues + Fixes?** What caused the decline? What specifically is management doing about it?
4. **Compensation?** Incentives aligned with shareholders? History of honesty?
5. **Catalysts?** Margin expansion, new leadership, regulatory clearance, demand drivers?

> "Show me the incentives, and I will show you the outcome." -- Charlie Munger

---

## Step 5: Valuation ([05-VALUATION](05-VALUATION.md))

**The Model:**

```
Revenue  x  FCF Margin  x  FCF Multiple  /  Shares  =  Price Target
```

- **Revenue:** Based on past growth + known catalysts + management targets
- **FCF Margin:** Where has it been? What cost actions justify recovery?
- **Multiple:** Start with industry baseline, adjust for quality, growth, leverage, risk
- **Shares:** Flat unless clear direction (buybacks shrinking, dilution growing)

**Hurdle: 30% CAGR required.**

One exception: 20%+ CAGR with a top-tier CEO over a 6+ year runway.

---

## Step 6: The Decision ([06-THE-DECISION](06-THE-DECISION.md))

**Score = three weighted inputs:**

| Input | Weight | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Reasonable Worst Case | 45% | Where does the stock go if things go wrong? |
| Base Case Probability | 40% | How likely is the base case or better? |
| Base Case CAGR | 15% | What annual return does the base case imply? |

**Note:** Downside risk is on a curve -- going from 20% to 30% downside penalizes more than the raw numbers suggest (~1.5x).

**Three scenarios for deploying capital:**

1. **Have cash, want the theme** -- Compare new idea's score to best existing position with room to add
2. **No cash, want the theme** -- Compare to weakest holding. Must be clearly better (40+ point score gap to replace, or existing position has <15% forward returns)
3. **No cash, maxed on theme** -- Compare to weakest position within that theme only

---

## Step 7: Position Sizing ([07-POSITION-SIZING](07-POSITION-SIZING.md))

The score determines the size. Higher score = larger position.

**Rules:**
- Max 12 positions
- Max 15% leverage (scales up in crashes: 10% drop = 20%, 15% drop = 25%, 25% drop = 30%)
- Max 50% of portfolio in a single catalyst/theme
- Keep emergency capital for drawdowns of 50%+

---

## Step 8: After the Buy ([08-AFTER-THE-BUY](08-AFTER-THE-BUY.md))

**Track continuously:**
- Are catalysts showing up?
- Is management saying one thing and doing another?
- Are margins shifting in unexpected ways?
- Are competitors pulling ahead?
- Which macro/geopolitical events actually move the needle?

**Beat fear with information, not willpower.**

**Sell when:**
1. Target price reached and forward returns don't meet hurdle rate
2. Rapid price move causes forward returns to drop below 10-15%/year
3. Fundamental change breaks the thesis

**After closing:** Review original thesis vs. what actually happened. Find mistakes and blind spots.
